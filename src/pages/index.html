Sound tied to places, made together

Roundware is a platform for participatory audio. People walk a map, hear the voices and sounds recorded where they stand, and add their own. Artists, museums, schools and communities have used it since 2008.

Start a project free How it works

What a project can do

Sound in place

Recordings are pinned to locations or drawn areas. As listeners move, what they hear changes with where they are.

Everyone contributes

Participants record from their phone or browser, answer your questions, and add photos or text. You choose what goes live.

Your shape, your sound

Sound walks, installations, oral histories, classroom projects: tags, menus, background sound and playback rules fit each one.

Any language

Every word participants see can be translated, and each project can offer several languages at once.

What participants see

A web app with your project's look. Nothing to install. It works on phones and computers, and on a page of your own as an embed.

Open source, and yours to take with you

The Roundware software is free and open source (AGPL), and has been since 2008. Run it on your own servers, or let us run it for you here. Either way, you can export everything at any time, recordings and all, and take it elsewhere.

roundware.org The code on GitHub

A small operation, run in the open

Roundware is run by one person, with no outside funding. There's no advertising and no selling of data, and nothing you upload trains AI. What we do well, and what we can't do yet, is written down plainly in How Roundware is run, so you can decide whether it fits your project.
