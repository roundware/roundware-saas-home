# Participant Terms — Template

**Draft template.** Organizations: this is a starting point for the terms your participants agree to before contributing. Change it to fit your project. If you broadcast contributions, ask a lawyer whether it covers what you do. Text in [brackets] is for you to fill in or choose.

This template keeps ownership with participants and gives the organization a license to use their contributions. If your organization needs to own contributions outright, replace section 2. Roundware doesn't decide this; you do.

## 1. What you're agreeing to

By contributing, you agree to these terms. Please read them; they're short.

## 2. Who owns your contribution

You do. "Your contribution" means the recording, photo, text and answers you send.

You give [ORGANIZATION] permission to use your contribution for [PROJECT NAME], without payment, worldwide, for as long as we keep it. That includes:

- [playing it on the project's site and map, and on share pages;]
- [broadcasting it, for example in a podcast or on radio;]
- [publishing it, for example on our website or social media;]
- [using it in exhibitions or installations;]
- [keeping it in our archive;]
- editing it for length or clarity, without changing its meaning;
- letting Roundware store and play it for us.

You can still use your contribution however you like yourself, and give others permission to use it too.

## 3. What's public

[Choose one:]
- [Contributions are public: anyone visiting the project can hear them and see where on the map they were made.]
- [Contributions are seen only by our team. We may choose some to share publicly, as section 2 says.]

Your name and email, if you give them, are **never** public. Only our team sees them, and we use them only to [contact you about your contribution / credit you, if you ask us to].

## 4. Changing your mind

You can ask us to remove your contribution at any time. Write to [CONTACT]. We'll stop using it from then on and delete it. If it was already broadcast, published or exhibited, we can't take that back, but we won't use it again.

## 5. Your promises

When you contribute, you promise that:

- it's your own, or you have the right to share it;
- anyone else who can be heard or seen in it agreed to be included;
- it doesn't include music or other material you don't have the right to share;
- it isn't hateful, threatening, or harmful to anyone.

We may choose not to use, or to remove, any contribution.

## 6. Young people

If you're under [13 in the USA / 16 in parts of Europe], please ask a parent or guardian before you contribute; they need to agree to these terms for you. [For school projects: your school has asked your parent or guardian already.]

## 7. Your data

We decide how your contribution and details are used, as these terms say. Roundware stores and plays them for us. How Roundware handles data is in its [Privacy Policy](privacy.md). To see, correct or delete what we have about you, write to [CONTACT].
